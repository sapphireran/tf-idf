Winter constellations rise early over the frozen lake. Orion stands
with a belt of three bright stars, and Sirius follows like a distant
beacon. A backyard telescope finds the Orion nebula as a pale thumbprint
in the sword. The observer stamps cold feet and waits for the sky to
steady. Gemini and Taurus complete the seasonal map. A meteor briefly
scratches the dark, then the quiet returns. Names of stars feel older
than the town below them. The observer logs magnitude, seeing, and the
hour, then packs the telescope before frost welds the tripod shut.
