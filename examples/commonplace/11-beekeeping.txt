# A queenright check

Smoke first, then one slow lift of the outer cover so the colony does not
decide this is a raid. I am looking for brood in all stages, a queenright
pattern, and no more varroa than last month's alcohol wash suggested.

A super of honey can wait. Brood frames cannot. Eggs standing like tiny
commas mean the queen was here two days ago even if I never spot her. Drone
comb on the edges is expected; a sudden explosion of drones is a story I
do not like. The smoker goes out twice because I packed the fuel too tight.

I am a careful amateur. The bees are not a metaphor and they are not a
hobby I get to neglect in July. They are a livestock question that happens
to have wings.
