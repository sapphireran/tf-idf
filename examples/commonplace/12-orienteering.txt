# Attack point before the control

The map is 1:10,000 and the forest is not impressed. I thumb-compass along
a ditch, pick an attack point at the boulder cluster, and only then hunt
the control flag. Going straight at the circle is how you lose ten minutes
in a reentrant that all look like cousins.

Declination on this map is already drawn into the north lines, which I still
double-check like a superstitious person. Pace counting gets me close;
terrain association gets me honest. A catching feature beyond the control
saves the leg when I overshoot.

I orienteer slowly. The sport rewards people who read the ground as a
sentence. I am still sounding out the words, but I no longer punch the
wrong flag just because it is orange.
