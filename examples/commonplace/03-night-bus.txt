# Last night bus

The last night bus is a different animal from the six o'clock commuter run.
Fluorescent tubes buzz. Someone eats a paper tray of fries two seats forward.
The driver knows every request stop after midnight and still waits a full
breath at the empty ones.

I take the transfer at the concrete island where three routes pretend to
meet. The ticket punch is gone; the validator beeps like a tired metronome.
A student sleeps against a window that has condensed into a private weather
system. At the terminus the bus kneels, the doors fold, and the depot cats
appear from under the fuel island.

I like the last run because it is honest about time. Nobody on it is
optimizing a day. We are just being delivered, slightly late, to wherever
the timetable still believes we live.
