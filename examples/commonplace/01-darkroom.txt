# Darkroom nights

I still keep a wet darkroom in the spare bathroom, even though everyone I know
has moved to a digital contact sheet. The enlarger is a battered Beseler that
throws a slightly warmer cone of light than the Ilford Multigrade filter pack
expects. I tape a scrap of black paper over the safelight cracks and mix fresh
Dektol in the first tray.

The ritual is the point. Film goes through developer, stop, and fixer; paper
gets the same three-bath waltz. I dodge a hot forehead with a cardboard wand
and burn the corners of a muddy beach negative until the grain sits where I
want it. Hypo-clear and a long wash keep the highlights from staining later.

When a print is right I can smell the acetic stop bath and hear the enlarger
fan. No histogram, no undo. Just density, contrast, and the patience to throw
the first six sheets away.
