# Dialing a stubborn puck

Eighteen grams in, something like thirty-six out, and the machine still
tells on me. Channeling shows up as a pale jet from one side of the basket
and a blonding shot that tastes like hot paper. I stir with a WDT tool,
tap the portafilter, and pretend the distribution is even.

Ratio is the polite variable. Grind, humidity, and the mood of the burrs
are the rude ones. I keep a notebook: time to first drop, total time, whether
the puck had a dead spot when I knocked it out. A good shot is syrup for a
second and then just coffee. A bad one is a negotiation I should have
stopped at the first blond streak.

I am not a barista. I am a person who owns one small machine and refuses to
let it win every morning.
