# Tuning a stubborn nib

The gold nib on the old Vacumatic has a baby-bottom: both tines kiss the
paper at once and the ink refuses to start. I look at the slit under a
loupe, then take a 0.3 micron micromesh stick to the tipping with more
caution than skill.

A fountain pen is a small hydraulic argument. The feed has fins that should
hold a reserve; the converter or the sac supplies it; the breather hole is
not decorative. Flex is not the same as softness. If the tines are misaligned
you get railroad tracks. If the slit is too tight you get a dry, virtuous
line that nobody wants to write with.

I flushed the iron-gall from the last fill and put in a tame blue-black.
The first page after a tune is always a little vain. Then the pen becomes
furniture again, which is the actual goal.
