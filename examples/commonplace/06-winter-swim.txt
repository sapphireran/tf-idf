# Winter swim off the jetty

Cold shock is a gasp you cannot negotiate with. I walk in to the ribs, wait
for the first spike to pass, and then start an easy breaststroke along the
jetty so I can stand up if the afterdrop arrives early.

Neoprene booties are vanity I have learned to keep. Brown fat is the
cheerful science story; the practical story is a warm hat on the walk back
and a thermos that was filled before I left the kitchen. I do not stay in
long enough to feel heroic. Ten minutes, maybe twelve if the wind is
offshore and the water is only biting, not chewing.

People ask about discipline. It is closer to curiosity. The harbor looks
different when you are in it, and the day afterwards is unusually quiet in
the head.
