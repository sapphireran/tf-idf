The schooner left the harbor on the morning tide.
Kelp drifted past the dark hull. A lighthouse blinked across the channel.
The crew coiled wet rope on the deck while tidewater slapped the pilings.
Wind pressed the mainsail. The schooner leaned, cleared the sand bar,
and took the open swell. Gulls followed the wake. The lighthouse
shrank to a pale tick. On the return the tide would be against them,
so the crew packed the spare canvas before noon. Kelp wrapped a
piling back in the harbor. The schooner kept its heading toward deeper water.
