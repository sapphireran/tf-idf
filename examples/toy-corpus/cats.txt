Miso the calico cat hunted moths on the porch after dusk.
The cat crouched by the lantern. Moths circled the warm lantern glass.
Miso leaped once, missed, and watched a moth escape into the garden hedge.
Night insects clicked in the hedge. The calico cat licked a paw, then
waited beside the lantern again. A second moth brushed the glass.
Miso did not leap. The cat only watched, tail ticking on the porch boards.
Later the lantern burned lower. Moths thinned. The calico cat curled
against the door and kept one eye on the hedge where the first moth vanished.
