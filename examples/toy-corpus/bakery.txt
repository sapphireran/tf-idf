The baker scored the sourdough loaves before dawn.
Rye dust covered the bench. The oven roared behind the rack.
Crust cracked as the first loaves cooled. A neighbor bought the warm rye
and left rye flour footprints to the door. The baker fed the starter,
shaped another sourdough round, and slid it toward the oven stone.
Steam fogged the shop window. The second batch of sourdough sang
when the crust split. The baker brushed rye dust from the bench
and set the cooled loaves in the window for the morning street.
