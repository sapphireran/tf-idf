The observatory dome opened onto a clear ridge.
A telescope tracked the comet past a faint nebula. Frost filmed the eyepiece.
The astronomer logged the comet tail and the nebula glow in a damp notebook.
Wind shook the dome slats. The telescope held its lock on the faint comet
while the nebula drifted at the field edge. Near dawn the astronomer
closed the dome, capped the telescope, and walked the ridge path
with the notebook tucked under one arm. The comet would return
on the next clear night. The nebula would still be there.
